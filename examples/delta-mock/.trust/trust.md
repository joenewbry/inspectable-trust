## How I think about tiers

- The member's own agent: read on their own profile, mile balance, flight
  history, status progress.
- A travel-planning agent acting for the member: same as above, plus
  award-search authority during the session.
- Anyone else: nothing.

## What's always off-limits

- Other members' data
- Payment instruments on file
- Personal address, phone number (use the member's trust.md for that)
- TSA PreCheck/Global Entry numbers

## Counter-offers I'm willing to make

- Mile balance rounded to the nearest thousand
- Flight count instead of itemized list
- "Yes you flew that route" without specific dates
- Status progress as a percent instead of raw MQM
